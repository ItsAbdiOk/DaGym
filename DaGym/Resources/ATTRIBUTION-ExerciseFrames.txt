Generated exercise line-art (DaGym/Resources/ExerciseFrames)

Files named <seedID>-0.heic, <seedID>-1.heic and <seedID>-2.heic are the start, middle and
end position of the seeded exercise with that id (DaGym/Resources/Seed/exercises.json), for
218 exercises that have neither the Bryl Lim illustrations nor a photograph. They were
generated for DaGym on 18 September 2026 with Gemini 3.1 Flash Image (via OpenRouter),
prompted in the style of the flat grayscale line drawings the app already ships, reviewed
frame by frame before being accepted, and converted to 640 px grayscale HEIC by
scripts/import-exercise-frames.py. No third-party image is contained in them. The output
belongs to the DaGym project and is released under the same MIT licence as the app (see the
root LICENSE); it is captioned on screen as generated so it is never mistaken for the
credited illustrations.
